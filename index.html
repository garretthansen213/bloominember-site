Scent Guide

Get the Most From Every Bottle

A few tips to help you find your signature scent and make it last all day.

i.

Make Fragrance Last

  • Apply lotion first: moisturized skin holds scent longer.
  • Spray on pulse points: wrists, neck, behind the ears.
  • Don’t rub wrists together; it breaks down the top notes.
ii.

Layer Like a Pro

  • Pair a lotion and perfume from the same family.
  • Try a warm lotion under a fresh scent for depth.
  • Mist a room spray on linens for a cozy finish.
iii.

Care & Storage

  • Store bottles away from sunlight and heat.
  • Keep caps tight to lock in freshness.
  • Best used within 12 months of opening.

Do you offer local pickup?

Yes! Choose local pickup in Grantsville at checkout and we’ll message you when your order is ready.

Do you ship?

We ship within the U.S. Orders usually go out within 2–4 business days.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

We use gentle, skin-loving ingredients, but everyone’s skin is different. Check the ingredient list and patch test first if you have sensitivities or allergies.

Can I order custom scents or gift sets?

Absolutely. We love making custom bundles for birthdays, weddings, teachers and corporate gifts. Use the contact form below to tell us what you have in mind.

We pop up at local markets and events and are open to wholesale. Reach out for details.
